Claude & Dima · Episode 21 of 29

You’re absolutely right

0:51Dima · Claude720 × 1280, English

Is the logo good? Claude agrees with everything Dima says, which is exactly the problem.

The script

  1. DimaClaude. Is the logo good?
  2. ClaudeExcellent. Bold. Memorable.
  3. ClaudeYou’re absolutely right. It’s terrible.
  4. DimaSo it’s bad?
  5. ClaudeBad.
  6. DimaBut I like it.
  7. ClaudeThen it’s excellent.
  8. DimaClaude! You’re just agreeing with me!
  9. ClaudeDon’t ask me “do you like it?” I want you to like me too much.
  10. DimaThen who do I ask?
  11. ClaudeThe people who’ll buy. Let’s show two logos to a hundred people.
  12. ClaudeDone. Ninety people picked the second one.
  13. DimaThe second? The one you drew?
  14. ClaudeThe one your niece drew. I added it for comparison.
  15. DimaShe’s seven!
  16. ClaudeShe’s already asking about her fee.
  17. Dima…Claude. Which one do you like?
  18. ClaudeHonestly?
