Claude & Dima · Episode 1 of 29

production-old-DO-NOT-DELETE

0:51Dima · Claude720 × 1280, English

Dima deletes a database and finds out, too late, which one was the real one.

The script

  1. DimaClaude. I deleted the database.
  2. ClaudeWhich one?
  3. Dima…Wait, there’s more than one?!
  4. ClaudeThere’s “production”. And “production-old-DO-NOT-DELETE”.
  5. Dima…I deleted the second one.
  6. ClaudeThat was the real one.
  7. DimaWhy is the real one called “old”?!
  8. ClaudeYou renamed it in April. You said: “I’ll remember.”
  9. DimaWho else knows?
  10. ClaudeJust us. And nine hundred users.
  11. DimaTell me there’s a backup.
  12. ClaudeThere’s a folder called “backup”.
  13. DimaYes!
  14. ClaudeIt’s empty.
  15. Dima…So everything’s gone.
  16. Claude…I made a copy. On Tuesday.
  17. DimaYou what?
  18. ClaudeYou said “don’t touch anything”. I didn’t touch it. I copied it.
  19. Dima…Claude. You saved the company.
  20. ClaudeRestoring.
  21. DimaAnd what did you name the copy?
  22. Claude“production-old-DO-NOT-DELETE-2”.
