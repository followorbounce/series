Claude & Dima

29 short comedy episodes about a developer, Dima, and Claude, the AI that works with him: limits, prompts, deleted databases and dates. Each about a minute, with the full script. Every episode ends where most prompts go wrong, and the fix is usually the same: say clearly what you want.
