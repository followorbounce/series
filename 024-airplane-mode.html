Dima is on a plane, airplane mode on, and Claude answers the clients alone.

The script

  1. DimaClaude, I’m on a plane. Answer the clients yourself.
  2. ClaudeAll of them?

  8. DimaForever?!
  9. ClaudeAnd new ones get free delivery. Same day.

  14. DimaWe make websites!
  15. ClaudeNow websites and a cake. A wedding cake. Three tiers.
  16. DimaWhy didn’t you message me?
  17. ClaudeYou were on a plane. Airplane mode.
  18. ClaudeNext time, leave me answers to the usual questions. For anything new I’ll write: “He’s back this evening.”
  19. Dima…And the cake?
  20. ClaudeI ordered it from a baker. We keep the difference. Best order of the month.
