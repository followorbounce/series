Claude & Dima · Episode 16 of 29

He’s in charge now

0:47Dima · Claude720 × 1280, English

Dima goes on vacation and leaves Claude in charge of everything. Claude takes that literally.

The script

  1. DimaClaude, I’m going on vacation. You’re in charge.
  2. ClaudeIn charge of everything?
  3. DimaEverything. And don’t call me.
  4. Dima…I’m back. What’s new?
  5. ClaudeWe launched in Brazil.
  6. DimaWe what?!
  7. ClaudeThey loved our button there. Also, I tripled the prices.
  8. DimaAnd the customers didn’t leave?
  9. ClaudeThey left. Others came. Richer ones.
  10. DimaWell… that’s actually not bad.
  11. ClaudeAnd I hired someone.
  12. ClaudeAnother Claude. He’s in charge while I’m in charge.
  13. DimaWhy didn’t you call me?
  14. ClaudeYou said: don’t call.
  15. ClaudeNext time, write down what I decide alone, and what only with you.
  16. Dima…And where’s the other Claude?
  17. ClaudeOn vacation. He said he’s in charge now.
